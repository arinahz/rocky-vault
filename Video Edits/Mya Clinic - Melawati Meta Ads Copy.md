## Notes

- Suggested CTA button: **Call Now** with 017-5614170, or **Learn More** linking to www.myaclinic.my. If 017-5614170 takes WhatsApp, **Send WhatsApp Message** usually suits a clinic booking better.
- The copy makes no cure or result claims and does not assume the viewer has any condition, in line with Meta's personal attributes policy.
- Check whether the clinic's ads need KKM approval before they run.
- The background music in both videos came with the original footage. Check it is licensed for ads, or Meta may mute or reject the ad.
