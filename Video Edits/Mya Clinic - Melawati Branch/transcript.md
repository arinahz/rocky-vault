# Mya Clinic: Melawati branch opening (transcript & edit notes)

Source: IMG_1298.MOV (29.75s), a walkthrough from the street to the reception desk with voiceover and music already mixed in. Edited cut: 29.4s.
The original timeline is kept as shot. The music is baked into the same audio track as the voiceover, so cutting pauses would have broken the beat. The edit adds graphics, captions and SFX on top.

## Transcript

Hi everyone. Mya Clinic is now open at Melawati. You can find us next to Bank Islam and opposite of Public Bank. So don't forget to visit Mya Clinic and book your appointment with us today. We look forward to seeing you.

(Descript heard "My clinic" and "Maya Clinic"; captions use Mya Clinic.)
